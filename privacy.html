Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-26

Shipmate Systems LLC ("Shipmate," "we," "us") builds and operates customer-communication software for service businesses in the United States. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information, including information used for text messaging (SMS).

Information we collect

On behalf of our client businesses, we process contact information — name, email, and mobile phone number — that a person submits through a client's website inquiry form, and information from the follow-up phone calls we place about that inquiry.

How we use mobile numbers and SMS consent

We send SMS only to people who, during a live phone call, verbally request a specific link (such as a trial-purchase or visit-scheduling link) and verbally agree to receive it by text. We send only the link the person requested. We do not send marketing blasts or recurring messages. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are used solely to deliver the requested message on behalf of the relevant client business.

We do not share or sell your information

We do not sell personal information. No mobile information (phone numbers or SMS consent) is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. It is used only to provide the requested service on behalf of our client business.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out — you'll receive no further texts. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, and use reasonable measures to protect it.
